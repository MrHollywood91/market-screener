Crude oil's sharp single-session move above $93 is the most consequential macro data point in today's session beyond earnings. Supply discipline from OPEC+ — with Saudi Arabia and Russia maintaining voluntary output cuts — combined with geopolitical risk premiums in the Middle East corridor are the primary drivers. For equity markets, this is a double-edged development: Energy sector names (XOM, CVX, OXY, SLB) benefit directly, and the XLE ETF is among today's top-performing sector vehicles. Airlines, logistics firms (FDX, UPS), and consumer staples companies with high freight exposure face immediate margin headwinds.

Elon Musk has been deposed in related proceedings. Financial Exposure: Criminal liability exposure for a corporation of Tesla's scale is difficult to quantify but structurally significant; civil MDL settlements could range from $1B-$5B+. A criminal indictment of the company (distinct from individuals) would be an unprecedented and market-moving event. Market Implication: TSLA's already compressed multiple makes it sensitive to headline risk; any escalation in the criminal track would pressure the stock significantly.
