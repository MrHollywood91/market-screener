SEC vs. Ken Leech / Western Asset Management (WAMCO) — Cherry-Picking Fraud

Ken Leech, former Co-Chief Investment Officer at Western Asset Management (WAMCO), a subsidiary of Franklin Templeton (BEN), has agreed to a personal $3 million SEC fine to settle allegations that he engaged in systematic cherry-picking — selectively allocating profitable trades to favored accounts while dumping losing positions into less-favored client portfolios.
